# Contacting Support
Support hours are Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM IST. Support is available through the in-app chat and by email at support@homenest.example.
Phone support is not available. For urgent safety issues, switch off the product at the wall switch and raise a priority ticket in the app.
Installation visits are scheduled within 48 hours of a request in metro cities and within 5 days elsewhere.
Support will never ask for your password or OTP.
