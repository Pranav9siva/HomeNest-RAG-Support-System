# Energy and Specifications
The HomeNest Aero fan uses a BLDC motor that consumes 28 watts at top speed, compared with about 75 watts for a conventional ceiling fan.
The Aero has 6 speed levels, a sleep mode that gradually lowers the speed over 2 hours, and a boost mode that runs at top speed for 15 minutes.
The recommended operating voltage range is 150 V to 285 V. The product has built-in surge protection up to 400 V.
The Aero is available in 1200 mm and 1400 mm sweep sizes.
