# Wi-Fi Setup and Provisioning
The HomeNest app pairs with the product over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) first, then provisions your home Wi-Fi credentials to the device.
HomeNest devices support 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. 5 GHz networks are not supported and will not appear in the network list.
To enter pairing mode, switch the product on and off three times within 5 seconds. The status light will blink blue when pairing mode is active.
If provisioning fails, keep your phone within 2 metres of the device, make sure Bluetooth and location permissions are enabled in the app, and retry.
