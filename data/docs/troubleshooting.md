# Troubleshooting
If the fan does not start, check that the wall switch is on, then power cycle the product by switching it off for 30 seconds.
If the remote does not respond, replace the CR2032 coin cell battery in the remote and point it at the receiver window.
A fast red blinking light on the unit means a motor fault; switch it off and contact support, do not keep running the product.
If the fan wobbles, tighten all blade screws and check that the blades are attached to the correct motor arms.
To factory reset the product, hold the power button for 10 seconds until the light blinks white twice.
