# Returns and Refunds
You can return a HomeNest product within 10 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned product passes inspection.
Products that were installed or show signs of use are not eligible for a return, but may be covered by the warranty if defective.
Return shipping is free for defective or wrongly delivered products.
