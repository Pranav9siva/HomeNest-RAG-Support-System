# Warranty Policy
HomeNest products come with a 2-year standard warranty on the fan motor and a 1-year warranty on all other parts, starting from the date of purchase.
The warranty can be extended by 1 year for a fee of 499 rupees if you register the product within 30 days of purchase.
Damage caused by water ingress, power surges, or unauthorized repairs is not covered by the warranty.
To claim warranty you need the invoice and the product serial number printed on the back of the unit.
